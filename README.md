### 🌟 Featured Research & Capstone

#### 🎓 [California Housing Price Prediction Research Capstone & Whitepaper](https://github.com/mohanasanthibattula-stack/housing-price-prediction-capstone)
- **Whitepaper PDF**: [Download 10-Page Research Report (PDF)](https://github.com/mohanasanthibattula-stack/housing-price-prediction-capstone/blob/main/report/Capstone_Research_Report.pdf)
- **Notebook**: [Interactive Jupyter Workflow](https://github.com/mohanasanthibattula-stack/housing-price-prediction-capstone/blob/main/notebooks/capstone.ipynb)
- **Highlights**: Rigorous econometric modeling on 20,640 census block groups from the 1990 U.S. Census (Pace & Barry, 1997). Champion Histogram-Based Gradient Boosting Regressor achieved $R^2 = 0.8470$, RMSE = \$44,774, and MAE = \$29,733. Features 500-fold bootstrap uncertainty quantification, diagnostic residual analysis isolating the \$500,000 right-censoring ceiling effect, and permutation importance identifying Median Block Income as the primary driver (68% relative importance). Fully automated CI on Python 3.12.
